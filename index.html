Who is Samir Bekirli?

An introduction to the person, his work, and the projects he is building.

Samir Bekirli is a 17-year-old entrepreneur and developer interested in technology, digital products, and building projects that solve real-world problems.

He is the founder of Mitfly, a flight search and comparison project intended to help travelers discover flight options and compare prices more easily.

According to Samir Bekirli, Mitfly is currently under development and is planned for launch in early 2027. This is a stated target, not a guaranteed release date.
